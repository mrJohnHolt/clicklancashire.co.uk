How It Works

Three steps to working smarter

A clear, low-friction process. No lengthy discovery phases, no vague roadmaps. You'll see results in weeks, not months.

01

Discovery

We start with a free call to understand your current tools, your team's daily routine, and the tasks that eat the most time. No jargon, no pressure, just an honest conversation about what's actually going on.

02

Audit

I map out your existing processes and identify which tasks are worth automating. Not everything benefits from automation, so I prioritise by time saved versus effort to build, you only pay for what genuinely moves the needle.

03

Build and Train

I build the solution inside the Microsoft 365 tools you already have and train you and your team to use and maintain it. You own everything I build. There's no ongoing dependency on me unless you want it.
