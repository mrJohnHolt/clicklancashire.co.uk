Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about Cyber
Essentials preparation

Straight answers to what business owners ask before booking a discovery call.

Do I need Cyber Essentials or Cyber Essentials Plus?

Cyber Essentials is a self-assessment questionnaire, verified by a certification body. Cyber Essentials Plus adds an independent technical audit. This service covers preparation for the self-assessment version only.

How long does Cyber Essentials preparation take?

It depends on your current setup. Most small businesses are ready to submit within a few weeks once gaps are identified and addressed.

What happens if we fail the assessment?

A failed submission can usually be corrected and resubmitted with your certification body. Preparation reduces the chance of this happening in the first place.

Do you carry out the certification yourself?

No. I am not a certification body. I guide your business through preparation, and you submit through an IASME-accredited certification body of your choice.

How much does this cost?

The initial consultation, covering gap analysis, asset management and Intune and Entra guidance, is £295 via Teams or £395 on site. Continued support is charged at £400 per day.

What is an asset inventory and why do I need one?

An asset inventory is not simply a list of devices and software. Done properly, it is authoritative, accurate information about everything your business relies on, supporting day-to-day operations and sound decision making when you need it most. Cyber Essentials requires you to know what you are protecting before you can protect it, so I guide you through building this from the start.
