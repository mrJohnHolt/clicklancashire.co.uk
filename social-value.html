Who Can Benefit

The free website is for
anyone with a genuine need

The person commissioning the paid project chooses the recipient. Here is who qualifies.

Organisations
  • Registered charities operating in Lancashire
  • Non-profit organisations and community interest companies
  • Community groups without formal charity status but with a clear community purpose
  • Local sports clubs, youth groups, and volunteer-run societies
Individuals
  • People with a genuine financial need who cannot afford professional services
  • Young people getting started: a sole trader, freelancer, or creative just launching
  • People with disabilities who face additional barriers to building an online presence
  • Individuals referred by a recognised support organisation or community group
A valid reason is required

The recipient does not need to apply formally, but a genuine need or community purpose must be clear. If you are unsure whether your chosen cause qualifies, just ask and I will always give an honest answer.

Why We Do This

Putting something back into
the place we call home

Click Lancashire exists because of Lancashire. Every client, every project, every referral comes from this county, from its people, its businesses, and its communities. It has always felt right that some of the value created here should stay here, going directly to the causes and individuals who need it most but who cannot always access professional services.

This is not a marketing exercise. There is no certificate, no badge on a wall. It is simply a commitment: every time a business in Lancashire invests in its own growth, someone else gets a genuine lift too.
