Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about your
new website

The answers to what business owners ask most before getting started.

How long does it take to build my website?

Most projects are completed within an average of 4 weeks, from receiving your brief to going live. The exact timeline depends on how quickly you can supply copy, images, and feedback during the design stage.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing is fixed and agreed upfront before any work begins, so there's no hourly billing and no surprise invoices. Get in touch with details about your business and I'll put together a fixed quote.

What's included in the price?

Every project includes a custom design built around your business rather than a recycled template, a fully mobile-friendly build that works on phones, tablets, and desktops, and a fast, SEO-ready foundation so your site is discoverable from day one.

Do you handle hosting and domain setup?

Yes. Whether you're starting from scratch or switching from an existing site, I'll guide you through hosting, domain connection, and getting your new website online when you're ready to launch.

What happens after my site goes live?

Ongoing support is available with no contract and no monthly fees. If you need updates, a new page, or just someone to call, I'm here to keep your site fresh, secure, and working.
